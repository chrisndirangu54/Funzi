# Funzi adaptive platform expansion

All experience features attach to curriculum IDs and emit mastery evidence. The platform does not optimize for screen time.

## Intelligence core
Learning Twin 2.0 tracks mastery, evidence confidence, misconceptions, forgetting risk, reading level, languages, pace, accessibility and historically effective modes. The Mastery Evidence Engine weights diagnostics, quizzes, retrieval, tutor interactions, oral exams, projects, assignments, simulations and teach-back differently. The Misconception Graph links error patterns to prerequisite skills. The Learning Experience Router chooses the next intervention using mastery, misconceptions, available time, bandwidth, accessibility and measured content effectiveness.

## Learner experiences
Multimodal tutor; photo/handwriting analysis; progressive whiteboard; simulations; procedural learning worlds; branching illustrated stories; video timestamp recommendations; audio/podcast lessons; Teach Funzi mode; adaptive oral examinations; debate coach; real-world quests; classroom cooperative quests; curriculum-map unlocks; accessibility modes; multilingual/code-switching tutoring; offline/low-bandwidth delivery.

## CBE evidence
Project and competency portfolios store learner artifacts, reflection, curriculum skills, competencies, rubric evidence and teacher verification. AI may suggest rubric evidence but may not mark itself teacher-verified.

## Teacher, parent and school intelligence
Teacher intervention radar, lesson copilot, assignment differentiation and authoring/approval studio. Parent summaries explain gains, gaps and practical next actions rather than exposing internal model scores alone. School dashboards aggregate mastery by authorized cohorts. National observatory analytics must use privacy-preserving aggregation and minimum cohort thresholds.

## Pathways
Career graph connects skills to occupations and education pathways. Opportunity matching can later ingest verified scholarships, competitions and programmes. Recommendations must explain why a match exists and avoid high-stakes autonomous decisions.

## Experimentation
Every content resource has a content-effectiveness record. Experiments compare mastery gain, retention and completion—not clicks. The router can gradually favor modes that work for a learner while retaining exploration and teacher overrides.

## Delivery sequence
1. Intelligence core and evidence event schema.
2. Router and content-effectiveness telemetry.
3. Multimodal/teach-back/oral/simulation experiences.
4. CBE portfolio and teacher intervention workflows.
5. Parent/school dashboards.
6. Offline packs, multilingual delivery and lightweight messaging channels.
7. Career/opportunity graph and privacy-preserving observatory.
